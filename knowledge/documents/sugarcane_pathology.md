# Sugarcane (Ganna) Pathology & Agronomic Management (Haryana Protocol)

## Sugarcane - Red Rot (Colletotrichum falcatum)
- **Pathogen:** *Colletotrichum falcatum* (Fungus)
- **Severity Level:** Critical ('Cancer of Sugarcane')
- **Symptoms:**
  - Discoloration of 3rd or 4th leaf from top; leaf margins turn yellow and dry up.
  - Longitudinal splitting of infected cane reveals reddened internal pith tissues with distinct crosswise white patches.
  - Alcoholic, sour fermentation odor emanating from split stalks.
- **Management:**
  - Plant healthy certified setts treated with Carbendazim 50% WP (1 g/L water) or hot water treatment (52°C, 30 min).
  - Soil application of *Trichoderma harzianum* enriched farmyard manure @ 10 kg/acre.
  - Immediately uproot and burn infected stools; do not ratoon severely infected fields.

## Sugarcane - Rust (Puccinia melanocephala)
- **Pathogen:** *Puccinia melanocephala* (Fungus)
- **Severity Level:** Moderate
- **Symptoms:**
  - Elongated chlorotic flecks turning reddish-brown pustules on both leaf surfaces.
- **Management:**
  - Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 500 g per acre or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 200 ml per acre in 200 L water.
