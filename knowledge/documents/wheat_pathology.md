# Wheat (Gehun) Pathology & Agronomic Management (CCS HAU & ICAR Protocol)

## Wheat - Stripe / Yellow Rust (Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici)
- **Pathogen:** *Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici* (Obligate biotrophic basidiomycete fungus)
- **Causal Agent:** Airborne fungal urediniospores blown from Himalayan foothills into Haryana plains (Panchkula, Ambala, Yamunanagar, Kurukshetra, Karnal).
- **Severity Level:** Critical / Epidemic Potential
- **Symptoms:**
  - Bright yellow to orange-yellow powdery pustules arranged in distinct, narrow parallel stripes (linear rows) along the leaf blade veins.
  - Pustules contain thousands of powdery yellow urediniospores that leave a yellow powder on fingers upon touching.
  - Severe infection causes premature leaf desiccation, lodging, and shriveled grains with up to 70% yield loss.
- **Favorable Weather Conditions:**
  - Cool temperatures (10°C to 18°C) accompanied by persistent winter morning fog, dew, and intermittent drizzle (RH > 85%).
- **Organic & Biological Control:**
  - Foliar spray of *Trichoderma viride* or *Bacillus subtilis* (5 g/L) at vegetative stages.
  - Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or cold-pressed neem oil (5 ml/L) mixed with 0.1% surfactant upon first notice of yellowing.
- **Chemical Control (CCS HAU Hisar & ICAR Standard Recommendations):**
  - **First Line Defense:** Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt / Bumper / Radar) @ 200 ml per acre dissolved in 200 Liters of water (1.0 ml per Liter of water).
  - **Alternative Triazoles:** Tebuconazole 250 EC (Folicur) @ 200 ml per acre or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 200 ml per acre in 200 L water.
  - Repeat spray after 15 days if cloudy/foggy conditions persist and new yellow pustules emerge on the flag leaf.
- **Cultural Management & Prevention:**
  - Cultivate stripe-rust resistant wheat varieties recommended for Haryana: HD-3086 (Pusa Gautami), HD-3226 (Pusa Yashaswi), WH-1105, PBW-550, and DBW-187 (Karan Vandana).
  - Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilization (keep Urea within 110-120 kg/acre split into 3 doses).
  - Practice early Rabi sowing (completed between 25th October and 15th November).

## Wheat - Leaf / Brown Rust (Puccinia triticina)
- **Pathogen:** *Puccinia triticina* (Basidiomycete fungus)
- **Severity Level:** Moderate to High
- **Symptoms:**
  - Small, round to oval, scattered cinnamon-brown to orange-brown pustules appearing primarily on the upper leaf lamina without linear striping.
  - Unlike stripe rust, pustules are non-linear, irregularly distributed across the blade.
- **Favorable Weather:**
  - Moderate to warm temperatures (18°C to 25°C) with high relative humidity and dew.
- **Management:**
  - Propiconazole 25% EC @ 200 ml/acre in 200 L water or Mancozeb 75% WP @ 500 g/acre.
  - Destroy volunteer wheat plants and alternate hosts around field bunds.

## Wheat - Stem / Black Rust (Puccinia graminis f. sp. tritici)
- **Pathogen:** *Puccinia graminis f. sp. tritici* (Fungus)
- **Severity Level:** Critical
- **Symptoms:**
  - Elongated, ragged reddish-brown pustules erupting through the epidermis of stems, leaf sheaths, and glumes, turning black as teliospores form late in the season.
- **Management:**
  - Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 200 ml/acre at initial detection.

## Wheat - Healthy Crop Care
- Ensure balanced basal fertilization (NPK 150:60:40 kg/ha). Apply Zinc Sulfate 21% @ 25 kg/acre during field preparation.
- Execute timely irrigations at critical physiological growth stages: CRI (Crown Root Initiation at 21 days), Tillering (40-45 days), Jointing (60-65 days), Flowering (80-85 days), and Milking/Dough stage (100-105 days).
