# Apple Pathology & Orchard Management

## Apple - Apple Scab (Venturia inaequalis)
- **Pathogen:** *Venturia inaequalis* (Ascomycete fungus)
- **Severity Level:** High
- **Symptoms:**
  - Olive-green, velvety to dark brown circular spots on leaves and young fruit.
  - Leaves become distorted, puckered, and turn yellow before premature drop.
  - On fruit: Corky, brown scab lesions that crack open, facilitating secondary fungal rot.
- **Favorable Weather:**
  - Cool (15°C to 20°C), wet spring weather with prolonged leaf moisture (> 9 hours).
- **Organic Controls:**
  - Lime sulfur sprays during delayed dormant stage.
  - Wettable sulfur (3-4 g/L) or Copper soaps during pre-bloom to petal fall.
  - Shred or rake fallen autumn orchard leaves and spray urea (5%) on leaf litter to accelerate decomposition of overwintering pseudothecia.
- **Chemical Control:**
  - Captan 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Dodine 65% WP @ 1.5 g/L (Protective).
  - Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.3 ml/L or Myclobutanil 10% WP @ 0.5 g/L (Curative kick-back).
- **Prevention:**
  - Plant scab-resistant cultivars (e.g., Liberty, Prima, Enterprise, GoldRush).
  - Annual canopy pruning to maximize sunlight penetration and rapid foliage drying.

## Apple - Black Rot (Botryosphaeria obtusa)
- **Pathogen:** *Botryosphaeria obtusa* (Fungal)
- **Symptoms:**
  - "Frog-eye" leaf spots: Small purple specks expanding into circular lesions with light brown centers and purple margins.
  - Fruit rot: Firm, brown rotting areas expanding in concentric rings, turning completely black and mummified.
- **Management:**
  - Prune out dead wood, fire blight strikes, and cankers.
  - Captan 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Thiophanate-methyl 70% WP @ 1.0 g/L from pink bud through cover sprays.
