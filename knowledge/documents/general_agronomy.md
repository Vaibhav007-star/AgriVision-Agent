# General Agronomy, Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Hindi Glossary

## Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM) Principles
1. **Prevention First:** Resistant crop varieties, crop rotation, sanitation, certified seed.
2. **Monitoring & Microclimate Alert:** Check relative humidity (>80%) and temperatures. Fungal spores (blight, rust) require at least 6-8 hours of free leaf moisture to germinate.
3. **Biological & Organic Priority:**
   - Neem Kernel Formulation (Azadirachtin): Repels insect vectors (aphids, whiteflies) and disrupts fungal spore attachment.
   - Bio-fungicides (*Trichoderma*, *Bacillus*): Colonize root zones and leaf surfaces, outcompeting pathogenic fungi.
4. **Targeted Chemical Intervention:**
   - Always rotate fungicide FRAC groups (Fungicide Resistance Action Committee) to prevent pathogen resistance.
   - Strictly adhere to Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) to guarantee food safety.

## Hindi Agricultural Terms & Glossary (हिंदी शब्दावली)
- **Early Blight / Late Blight:** अगेती झुलसा (Early Blight) / पछेती झुलसा (Late Blight)
- **Rust:** गेरुआ / रतुआ रोग (Rust)
- **Leaf Spot / Scab:** पत्ती धब्बा रोग / स्कैब
- **Fungicide:** फफूंदनाशक
- **Bio-pesticide:** जैविक कीटनाशक / नीम का तेल
- **Crop Rotation:** फसल चक्र
- **Dosage Guide:** 2 ग्राम प्रति लीटर पानी में मिलाकर छिड़काव करें।
- **Pre-Harvest Interval:** तुड़ाई से पहले की प्रतीक्षा अवधि (PHI)।
