# Rice / Paddy (Dhaan) Pathology & Agronomic Management (CCS HAU & ICAR Protocol)

## Rice - Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae)
- **Pathogen:** *Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae* (Gram-negative bacterium)
- **Severity Level:** High
- **Symptoms:**
  - Water-soaked to yellowish-green undulating lesions starting from leaf tips or margins, expanding downwards into long straw-colored bleached stripes.
  - Early morning dew displays milky bacterial ooze beads drying into amber crusted droplets on leaf surfaces.
  - 'Kresek' phase causes rapid wilting and seedling death within 1-3 weeks after transplanting.
- **Favorable Weather:**
  - High humidity (> 75%), temperature 25-34°C, strong winds and rain showers.
- **Management:**
  - Spray Streptocycline @ 20 g per acre + Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 500 g per acre in 200 Liters of water.
  - Avoid top-dressing nitrogen while disease is active. Drain excess standing water for 3-4 days.

## Rice - Brown Spot (Bipolaris oryzae / Helminthosporium oryzae)
- **Pathogen:** *Bipolaris oryzae* (Fungus)
- **Severity Level:** Moderate
- **Symptoms:**
  - Dark brown, oval or sesame seed-shaped spots with a gray or whitish center and yellow chlorotic halo.
- **Management:**
  - Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 500 g/acre or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 200 ml/acre in 200 L water. Apply Potash (MOP) to correct nutritional stress.

---

## Rice - Leaf Smut (Entyloma oryzae)
- **Pathogen:** *Entyloma oryzae* (Fungus)
- **Severity Level:** Moderate
- **Symptoms:**
  - Small, angular, slightly raised leaden-black spots scattered on both sides of rice leaves.
- **Management:**
  - Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 120 g/acre or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 200 ml/acre.
