# Corn (Maize) Pathology & Agronomic Management

## Corn - Common Rust (Puccinia sorghi)
- **Pathogen:** *Puccinia sorghi* (Fungal obligate parasite)
- **Severity Level:** Moderate
- **Symptoms:**
  - Small, circular to elongate reddish-brown cinnamon pustules (uredinia) scattered across both upper and lower leaf surfaces.
  - Pustules rupture the leaf epidermis, releasing powdery golden-brown rust spores upon touch.
  - Chlorosis and necrosis of leaf tissue surrounding dense pustule clusters.
- **Favorable Weather:**
  - Cool to moderate temperatures (16°C to 25°C) and high humidity (> 95%).
- **Organic & Biological Control:**
  - Foliar application of sulfur dust or wettable sulfur (3 g/L).
  - Spray bio-fungicide *Bacillus amyloliquefaciens* (2.5 g/L).
- **Chemical Control:**
  - Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 ml/liter.
  - Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1.0 ml/L or Pyraclostrobin 20% WG @ 1.0 g/L applied at first sign of pustules prior to tasseling.
- **Cultural Management:**
  - Plant resistant hybrids containing Rp1-D or multi-gene resistance.
  - Early planting to avoid peak late-season airborne rust spore showers.

## Corn - Northern Leaf Blight (Exserohilum turcicum)
- **Pathogen:** *Exserohilum turcicum* (Fungal)
- **Symptoms:**
  - Long, elliptical cigar-shaped grayish-green to tan lesions (2.5 cm to 15 cm long) parallel to leaf veins.
  - In damp weather, dark olivaceous spore masses form inside the lesions.
- **Management:**
  - Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Azoxystrobin + Cyproconazole.
  - Deep tillage of crop residues and 2-year rotation with non-cereal crops (soybeans, legumes).

---

## Corn - Healthy Crop Care
- Ensure adequate nitrogen application at knee-high and silking stages.
- Maintain soil moisture during critical tasseling and grain filling windows.
