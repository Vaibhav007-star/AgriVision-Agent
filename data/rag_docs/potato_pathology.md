# Potato Crop Pathology & Agronomic Management

## Potato - Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- **Pathogen:** *Alternaria solani* (Fungal)
- **Severity Level:** Moderate
- **Symptoms:**
  - Dark brown, angular to circular spots with concentric rings on mature potato leaves.
  - Tuber lesions: Dark, sunken, circular lesions with raised purple-brown margins.
  - Gradual yellowing of lower foliage leading to premature leaf senescence.
- **Favorable Weather:**
  - High humidity cycles alternating between wet and dry conditions; temperatures 20°C - 28°C.
- **Organic Controls:**
  - Bio-fungicide spray of *Trichoderma harzianum* (5 g/L).
  - Neem seed kernel extract (NSKE 5%) or copper octanoate (soap).
  - Adequate soil potassium and balanced nitrogen to maintain plant vigor.
- **Chemical Treatment:**
  - Contact fungicide: Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Translaminar/Systemic: Pyraclostrobin 20% WG @ 1.0 g/L or Boscalid + Pyraclostrobin formulation.
- **Cultural Practices:**
  - Avoid moisture stress; maintain uniform soil moisture with sprinkler morning schedules so leaves dry before sunset.
  - Harvest mature tubers after vine killing to reduce skinning and spore inoculation.

## Potato - Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- **Pathogen:** *Phytophthora infestans* (Oomycete)
- **Severity Level:** Severe / Destructive
- **Symptoms:**
  - Dark, water-soaked, spreading necrotic lesions on leaves and stems.
  - White mildew fringe on the underside of leaves during cool, moist periods.
  - Infected tubers exhibit copper-brown or purplish granular dry rot beneath the skin extending into the flesh.
- **Favorable Weather:**
  - Temperatures 10°C - 20°C with relative humidity > 90% and continuous free moisture.
- **Organic Controls:**
  - Bordeaux mixture 1% or Copper Hydroxide (2.5 g/L) preventative sprays.
  - Strict culling and disposal of seed tubers showing dry rot or brown discoloration.
- **Chemical Control:**
  - Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L + Mancozeb @ 2.0 g/L.
  - Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Fluopicolide + Propamocarb Hydrochloride (Infinito) @ 1.5 ml/L.
- **Prevention:**
  - Use certified blight-free seed tubers (e.g., Kufri Girdhari, Kufri Khyati, Kennebec).
  - High hilling of rows (20-25 cm ridge height) to protect developing tubers from surface wash of spores.
  - Destroy cull piles before crop emergence.

## Potato - Healthy Crop Care
- Ensure deep loose sandy-loam soil with good drainage.
- Maintain soil pH 5.2 - 6.4 (slightly acidic helps suppress common scab).
- Balanced N-P-K (150:100:120 kg/ha) with micronutrient foliar sprays of Zinc and Boron.
