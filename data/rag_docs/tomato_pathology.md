# Tomato Crop Pathology & Agronomic Management

## Tomato - Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- **Pathogen:** *Alternaria solani* (Fungal)
- **Severity Level:** Moderate to High
- **Diagnostic Symptoms:**
  - Distinct dark brown to black circular lesions on older lower leaves.
  - Classic concentric rings creating a "target-board" or "bullseye" pattern.
  - Yellow chlorotic halo surrounding necrotic spots.
  - Premature defoliation, sunscald of fruits, and dark collar rot at stem base.
- **Favorable Weather Conditions:**
  - Warm temperatures (24°C to 30°C / 75°F to 86°F).
  - High relative humidity (> 80%) with heavy dew or frequent rainfall.
  - Prolonged leaf wetness duration (over 8-10 hours).
- **Organic & Biological Treatment:**
  - Spray cold-pressed pure Neem Seed Oil (5 ml per liter of water with 1 ml liquid soap emulsifier) every 7-10 days.
  - Apply bio-fungicide *Trichoderma viride* or *Trichoderma harzianum* (5-10 g/L) to soil and foliage.
  - Foliar spray of *Bacillus subtilis* formulation (2-3 g/L) during early emergence.
  - Immediately prune and burn/bury all lower infected leaves (bottom 30 cm of canopy).
- **Chemical Control & Fungicides:**
  - Protective contact spray: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/liter of water or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 3.0 g/liter.
  - Systemic curative spray for active spread: Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1.0 ml/L or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5-1.0 ml/L.
  - Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 7-14 days. Observe safety intervals before harvest.
- **Cultural Prevention & Next Cycle:**
  - Practice 3-year crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops (avoid potato, eggplant, pepper).
  - Use plastic or organic straw mulching to prevent soil spores splashing onto lower foliage.
  - Shift exclusively to drip irrigation; strictly avoid overhead sprinkler watering.
  - Maintain plant spacing of 60 cm x 45 cm for adequate cross-ventilation.

## Tomato - Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- **Pathogen:** *Phytophthora infestans* (Oomycete water mold)
- **Severity Level:** Critical / Emergency
- **Diagnostic Symptoms:**
  - Large, irregular water-soaked pale green to dark brown oily lesions on leaves and stems.
  - White, cottony fungal growth (sporangiophores) on the underside of leaves in damp morning conditions.
  - Rapid browning, shriveling, and death of entire foliage within days.
  - Firm, greasy, dark brown sunken rot on green and ripe tomato fruits.
- **Favorable Weather Conditions:**
  - Cool to moderate temperatures (12°C to 22°C).
  - High relative humidity (> 90%) and continuous cloudiness or rainfall.
- **Organic & Biological Control:**
  - Preventive Bordeaux mixture (1%) or Copper Hydroxide (2.0 g/L).
  - Bio-control *Pseudomonas fluorescens* (5 g/L) foliar spray.
  - Rapid removal and destruction of all severely infected plants to prevent field-wide epidemic.
- **Chemical Control:**
  - Systemic action: Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil MZ) @ 2.5 g/liter.
  - Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L.
  - Spray during dry foliage conditions with surfactant. Repeat after 7 days if weather remains overcast.
- **Preventive Practices:**
  - Plant certified disease-free transplants and blight-resistant hybrids (e.g., Mountain Magic, Defiant).
  - Destroy all solanaceous volunteer plants and cull piles in surrounding vicinity.

## Tomato - Leaf Mold (Passalora fulva)
- **Pathogen:** *Passalora fulva* (Fungal)
- **Diagnostic Symptoms:**
  - Pale green to yellowish diffuse spots on upper leaf surfaces.
  - Olive-green to velvety brown mold growth on lower leaf surfaces beneath the yellow spots.
- **Management:**
  - Increase greenhouse and canopy air circulation; reduce relative humidity below 80%.
  - Apply Potassium Bicarbonate (3 g/L) or Copper-based fungicides.

## Tomato - Healthy Care Guidelines
- Maintain soil pH between 6.0 and 6.8.
- Balanced fertilization: Avoid excessive nitrogen which promotes dense, humid foliage susceptible to fungal spores.
- Apply calcium-rich amendments (gypsum / lime) to prevent blossom end rot.
