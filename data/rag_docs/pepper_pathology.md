# Pepper (Bell) Pathology & Management

## Pepper Bell - Bacterial Spot (Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria)
- **Pathogen:** *Xanthomonas campestris pv. vesicatoria* (Bacterium)
- **Severity Level:** Severe
- **Symptoms:**
  - Small, water-soaked, angular, dark brown to black spots (1-3 mm) on leaves.
  - Lesions may develop a pale yellowish border; centers dry out and drop out, giving a "shot-hole" look.
  - Severe leaf drop leaving fruits exposed to sunscald.
  - Raised, scab-like warty brown spots on green and red bell pepper fruits.
- **Favorable Weather:**
  - Warm (24°C to 32°C), wet, windy conditions with driving rain and high humidity.
- **Organic & Bactericidal Controls:**
  - Copper Hydroxide (2.0 g/L) combined with bio-formulation *Bacillus subtilis* (2.5 g/L).
  - Streptomycin Sulfate + Tetracycline Hydrochloride (Streptocycline) @ 0.5 g/10 liters of water in agricultural emergency use where permitted.
  - Disinfect pruning tools with 70% isopropyl alcohol or 10% bleach between plants.
- **Cultural Management:**
  - Use hot-water treated certified disease-free seeds (50°C for 25 minutes).
  - Avoid touching or cultivating pepper rows when plants are wet with dew or rain.
  - Employ drip irrigation only; rotate out of Solanaceae crops for minimum 2 seasons.
