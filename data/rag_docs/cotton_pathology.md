# Cotton (Kapas / Narma) Pathology & Agronomic Management (Haryana Protocol)

## Cotton - Bacterial Blight / Angular Leaf Spot (Xanthomonas citri pv. malvacearum)
- **Pathogen:** *Xanthomonas citri pv. malvacearum*
- **Severity Level:** High
- **Symptoms:**
  - Angular, water-soaked translucent lesions delimited by leaf veinlets, turning reddish-brown to dark black.
  - Black arm phase attacks petioles and stems causing breaking and boll shedding.
- **Management:**
  - Spray Streptocycline @ 20 g per acre mixed with Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 500 g per acre in 200 L water.
  - Apply cold-pressed Neem Oil (5 ml/L) for sucking pest vector control (Whiteflies, Jassids).

---

## Cotton - Healthy Crop Care
- Ensure balanced NPK (80:40:40 kg/ha). Apply 4 foliar sprays of 2% Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) at flowering and boll development.
- Monitor whitefly populations to prevent Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV) transmission.
